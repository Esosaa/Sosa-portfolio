About

I design for systems
that earn trust

I'm a Senior Product Designer. Most of my work lives in fintech, logistics, and operations. These are spaces where the systems are complex, the stakes are real, and you can't afford to get the fundamentals wrong.

I don't start with how things look. I start with how they work, who they're for, and what needs to be true for people to trust them. The visual layer comes after that foundation is solid.

Focus

Fintech, Supply Chain, B2B SaaS, Operations

Approach

Systems thinking, user research, product strategy

Tools

Status
